<h1>My Summer Highlights</h1>

<p>Welcome to my website! This summer was full of fun activities and memorable trips with family and friends. Here are a few highlights from my summer break.</p>

<h2>Favorite Activities & Places</h2>

<h3>Hiking in Zion National Park</h3>
<p>I went on a <strong>week-long trip</strong> to <strong>Zion National Park</strong> with my siblings, and we did about <strong>five different hikes</strong>. Some of them were super long with <strong>over 1,000 feet of elevation gain</strong>, but the views were completely worth it. We were right in the middle of the valley surrounded by <strong>bright orange mountains</strong>, and we even got up close with wildlife—seeing <strong>mountain goats</strong> just <strong>10 feet away</strong> and <strong>deer</strong> only <strong>5 feet away</strong>.</p>

<h3>LA City Skyline at Night</h3>
<p>My sister and I drove up into the hills near <strong>Downtown LA</strong> to catch the <strong>sunset</strong> and check out the <strong>city skyline</strong>. On the drive up, we listened to my favorite album, <em><strong>good kid, m.A.A.d city</strong></em>, which made driving through LA with that sunset in the background feel incredible.</p>

<h3>Evening Sunset Walk</h3>
<p>It was a <strong>beautiful sunset walk</strong> near the ocean inside a <strong>nature reserve</strong>. While walking the trail, I spotted a <strong>rattlesnake</strong> and a <strong>huge bird</strong>. It ended up being a really nice, <strong>relaxing day</strong>.</p>
